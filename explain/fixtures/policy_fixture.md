# TEST FIXTURE — not the team's Relief Policy

Used only until `docs/relief_policy.md` exists. Same format: `### <id> <title>` then the clause text.

## 1 Scope

### 1.1 Disaster zones
A payment is treated as disaster-affected when it is made inside an officially declared disaster zone during the declared period, using the zone's severity tier.

## 3 Customers in a disaster

### 3.1 Displaced customers
Night-time payments, new or borrowed phones and large distance from the usual location are expected for displaced customers and must not on their own lead to a hold.

### 3.2 Essential merchants
Payments to pharmacies, clinics, grocery stores, fuel stations and telecom recharge are essential and get the highest urgency weight.

## 4 Uncertain payments

### 4.1 Review instead of freeze
When the fraud assessment is uncertain, the bank reviews the payment instead of freezing the account.

### 4.2 Survival stipend
During review, a survival stipend up to the stipend cap is released immediately and the remaining amount is held in escrow.

### 4.3 Escrow release
Escrowed money is released to the customer automatically within 48 hours unless fraud is confirmed by an analyst.

## 6 Fraud and mule rings

### 6.1 Clear fraud
Payments with a high calibrated fraud probability that outweighs the harm of holding them are held for analyst review.

### 6.2 Mule-ring override
Payments to accounts that collect many transfers and forward them quickly, share phones or networks with other accounts, or sit at the centre of a transfer ring are held regardless of the disaster context.

## 7 Customer rights

### 7.1 Appeal
Every customer can appeal a hold or review with one tap; appeals go to the front of the analyst queue.
